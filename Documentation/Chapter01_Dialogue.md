# Chapter 01 — revised dialogue

## intro

**ELIAS:** B-four. Same walls, same lock. If I want answers, I need people on my side.

**GUARD:** Vale! Yard is open. Stay out of trouble.

## reader

**TOMAS:** You are the new man in B-four. Tomas. Sit for a minute. Nobody survives this place alone.

**ELIAS:** Elias. I could use a friend. Or at least someone honest.

**TOMAS:** Try Niko. Blue Meridian. Malik runs his crew. The Rooks stay across the yard. Do not wander into their business.

## cook

**SAMIR:** Oi, new guy. Grab that end. These trays weigh a ton.

**ELIAS:** Got it. Where do you want them?

**SAMIR:** Right here. Thanks. Samir. You need a hot meal, come find me.

## fixer

**WORKSHOP CONTACT:** Jonah. I fix whatever the guards break. Which is just about everything.

**ELIAS:** Tell me the routine. Searches, roll call... the things I should know.

**WORKSHOP CONTACT:** Whistle means count. Yard stays open till then. Play a hand, talk to people. And keep your eyes on Harrow.

## conceal

**GUARD:** Enough! Back away. Both of you!

**NIKO:** Just a disagreement, officer. It is over.

**ELIAS:** Keep walking. I have it.

## thanks

**NIKO:** Harrow finds that blade, I disappear into isolation. You just saved me.

**ELIAS:** Nobody saw. Let us keep it that way.

**NIKO:** You had no reason to help. I will remember that. Come on. Malik should meet you.

## gang_oath

**MALIK:** Niko does not owe favors lightly. What do you want, Vale?

**ELIAS:** A place to stand. People I can trust.

**MALIK:** Then stand with us. Blue Meridian. We protect our own. But trust goes both ways.

**ELIAS:** Understood. I am with you.

**MALIK:** Rafi gives you our mark. After that, get the blade off the yard. Keep it in your cell.

## tattoo

**RAFI:** Give me your arm. First mark always stings. Do not move.

**ELIAS:** I can take it.

**RAFI:** There. Blue Meridian. Go hide that knife before Harrow changes his mind.

## stash

**ELIAS:** Under the mattress. Out of sight. Nobody touches it but me.

## phone_gift

**NIKO:** You did what Malik asked. Here. He wants you to have this.

**ELIAS:** A phone? How did you get it in?

**NIKO:** Less you know, better you sleep. Keep it quiet. Malik will call.

## phone_call

**MALIK:** Vale. Signal is good. Keep this number to yourself.

**ELIAS:** I am listening.

**MALIK:** Rooks are leaning on our people again. Speak to their man in the yard. Leave the blade in your cell.

## rival

**IRON ROOKS:** Meridian gave you a new shirt and now you think you own the yard?

**ELIAS:** Nobody owns it. Leave our people alone.

**IRON ROOKS:** Come over here and say that.

## arrest

**GUARD:** Hands up! All of you. Fighting stops now!

**GUARD:** Separate cells. Every one of you. Twenty-four hours in isolation.

**ELIAS:** All right. Easy. I am coming.

## isolation

**ELIAS:** Twenty-four hours. Nothing to do but close my eyes.

## wake

**GUARD:** Vale! Get up. Your time is done. You have a visitor.

## guard_visit

**GUARD:** Your brother is waiting. Come out. Walk with me.

**ELIAS:** Theo? He never comes here. What happened?

## brother_visit

**THEO:** Elias... I am sorry. I did not know where else to go.

**ELIAS:** Hey. Breathe. Tell me what happened.

**THEO:** They took Dad. The Cinder Syndicate. The same men who put you in here.

**ELIAS:** Look at me. Was he alive when you last saw him?

**THEO:** Yes. But they said next week... if we do not pay, they will kill him.

**ELIAS:** Find somewhere safe. Stay off your phone. I am bringing him home.

**THEO:** Please. I cannot lose you both.

## reflection

**ELIAS:** They framed me. Locked me away. Now they have my father.

**ELIAS:** Six days. I need a way out. Then I find him. Then we settle this.

## escape_plan

**ELIAS:** Malik. The men who framed me took my father. I have six days. I need to get out.

**MALIK:** Everyone here wants out. Wanting it does not open a door.

**ELIAS:** Then help me build a way. Get enough people working with us. When I leave, you leave too.

**MALIK:** Cooperation. Access. The right moment. Bring me enough of those, and you have a partner.

**ELIAS:** We leave together. Before next week.

**MALIK:** Sit down. Let us work out who we can trust.

## yard_table

**TOMAS:** Pull up a seat. Card table is for passing time. This one is for listening.

**ELIAS:** What do the guards miss?

**TOMAS:** Things right in front of them. Who talks to who. Who owes who. Learn the people first.

## poker_welcome

**SAMIR:** A friendly hand? Five-card draw. Keep what you like. We play for bragging rights.

## ambient_tomas

**TOMAS:** Counts in ten minutes. Plenty of time for another hand.

## ambient_samir

**SAMIR:** My cooking is better than my cards. That is not saying much.

## ambient_jonah

**WORKSHOP CONTACT:** Work detail at the next whistle. Do not be late.

## ambient_niko

**NIKO:** Keep your voice down. Harrow hears more than you think.

## ambient_malik

**MALIK:** Every favor gets remembered. Good and bad.
