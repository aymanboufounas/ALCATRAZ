# Supplied island startup and asset locations

Pressing Unity's Play button now starts `SC_AlcatrazSuppliedIsland`, even when an older scene was left open. Character and supplied-model preparation runs before entering Play. The original scenes remain available through the explicit Alcatraz menus; choosing them switches Play to the current scene for that editor session. **Alcatraz > Start Play on Supplied Island** restores the default.

The supplied Alcatraz scan is the exterior. Its native coastline mesh and textures are used directly. The Little Bird helicopter now stands on a level pad beside the starting courtyard rather than at the remote waterfront. Enter/Explore starts beside it. The pause/title menu has Cellhouse, Helipad and Coastal house destinations, and the island map marks the helicopter and house.

The nine uploaded packages remain included: Alcatraz, police, animated police dog, helmet, vest, Little Bird, coastal house, 10mm pistol and revolver. Police, dog and tactical gear are by the cellhouse entrance; the house is on its coastal platform. Keys 3 and 5 equip the supplied pistol and revolver. No helicopter flight system is claimed.

Static triangle sampling checks the courtyard spawn and all nine helipad floor samples. Unity import, rendering, runtime physics and a Windows build still require validation in Unity.
