# Factory, speaker array and radio uploads

Three original uploads are imported as native prefabs under
`Assets/Game/Supplied/Resources/AlcatrazExtras` and connected to the workshop
annex. Choose **Workshop & recreation annex**, then walk north through the
eight-metre opening into the factory yard. The factory is to the left; the speaker
array and radio table are to the right.

| Source ZIP | Native model | Source/native triangles | Imported textures |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| `old-factory-free.zip` | Factory | 113,689 / 113,689 | 11 |
| `speaker-array.zip` | SpeakerArray | 802 / 802 | 4 |
| `lowpoly-radio.zip` | Radio | 1,070 / 1,070 | 1 |

These are the supplied meshes, not replacement primitives. Their conversions use
`--preserve-quality`: no triangle simplification, no texture downscaling and
lossless PNG encoding. Batching only splits index buffers into manageable files.
Source texture dimensions are recorded individually in each model's `Source.json`.
The original ZIPs are retained byte for byte in `Documentation/SourceArchives`.
Full-detail conversion is also the default for these three model names, so a later
import does not silently switch them back to the older optimized mode.

The radio's supplied `T_Radio.jpg` atlas is explicitly connected because the FBX
omits its texture binding. The speaker uses the original glTF texture graph instead
of Assimp's reordered embedded-image indices. Its diffuse and normal maps retain
512-pixel resolution; the two-pixel metallic/roughness map is retained and converted
to Unity's metallic/smoothness channel layout. Its double-sided material is retained.
Custom source material extensions, lighting and a source application's renderer
are not reproduced exactly by the standard URP material conversion.

The factory and speaker are static collision scenery. The radio is a static prop
on a table. This change does not add radio playback or speaker audio.

## Validation

`Tools/validate_extra_assets.py` now checks all 26 catalog entries, resource
registration, native triangle counts, material/texture references and images. For
these three imports it also checks unchanged source triangle counts, full texture
dimensions and retained original archive hashes. The three retrieved ZIPs passed
SHA-256 and CRC verification. Results are in
`Validation/FactoryAudioAssetsChecks.json`.
Full native integrity validation also passed with zero errors across 2,597 native
files; its report is `Validation/FactoryAudioIntegrity.json`. C# grammar passed for
all 73 files. Reimporting all three assets reproduced the same generated bytes.
The quality validator was checked against deliberately reduced texture resolution
and missing triangles and rejected both cases.

Labelled [factory](FactoryAudioPreviews/Factory.png),
[speaker](FactoryAudioPreviews/SpeakerArray.png) and
[radio](FactoryAudioPreviews/Radio.png) previews render the converted native meshes
and base-color textures offline. They are not Unity captures and do not reproduce
the full URP material response. Recreate them with
`python Tools/render_factory_audio_previews.py`.

The yard floors overlap by one metre at the original annex boundary. The north
opening, perimeter and recovery bounds include the new models. Existing warehouse
checks still cover both warehouse doors and barrels.

Unity import, visuals, swept collision, performance and player builds remain
unverified because the editor is unavailable here.

## Two uploads remain blocked

`chevrolet-kodiak-c70.zip` and `an-abandoned-garage.zip` exceed the attachment
transfer tool's 32 MiB limit. They could not be downloaded or imported, and no
substitute models were created. Provide each ZIP as split parts smaller than
32 MiB so the original bytes can be reassembled and imported.
