# Revision 08 Unity verification

Not performed in the authoring environment; Unity Editor is unavailable.

1. Open the repository in Unity 6000.0.62f1. Resolve packages and let FBXs import. Inspect the Console for C# and shader errors.
2. Choose Alcatraz > Prepare Characters, then Alcatraz > Open Island. Enter Play Mode and check Elias is the orange prisoner with the revised head texture; Harrow has the separate navy outfit.
3. Walk the yard, all three galleries, dining hall, showers, both industries, dock, switchbacks and new western coastal trail. Check support, turns, parapets, camera collision and that added scenery leaves paths usable.
4. Look toward the bay at low and high camera angles. Check camera depth, shallows/foam, sky, shadows and post-processing. Watch the Console and adjust rendering for the target PC after measuring frame rate.
5. Play Chapter 01 from a fresh save. Test knife pickup and mandatory stash, success/failure states, phone reward, rival fight, arrest, condensed isolation, Theo conversation, poker, respect changes and checkpoint reload.
6. Test NPC routines and footsteps. Inspect Elias's face UVs and skin/hair transitions at close range.
7. Install Windows Build Support and choose Alcatraz > Build Windows x64. Run Builds/Windows/AlcatrazEscape.exe and repeat the main flows.
